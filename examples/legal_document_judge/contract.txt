FICTIONAL SERVICE AGREEMENT
Educational fixture only; not a contract template.

Clause 1 - Term
The agreement runs for twelve months from the service start date.

Clause 2 - Termination without cause
After the first 90 days of service, the customer may terminate this agreement
without cause by giving the provider at least 30 days' written notice.

Clause 3 - Fees on termination
The customer must pay fees for services supplied through the effective
termination date. No additional early termination fee applies.

Clause 4 - Termination for breach
Either party may terminate if the other party commits a material breach
and fails to remedy it within 15 days after receiving written notice
describing the breach.
